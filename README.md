One line roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday, for the
[Snooze Button Roast](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-snooze-button-roast-14cbafc6)
bounty on make.fun.

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, no network, no randomness) writes the line
deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My alarm clock rings at 6 AM on a Saturday like the weekend personally owes it money.
